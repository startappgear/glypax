The Short Version

  • No account, no login. You do not create an account. The App keeps an anonymous local profile on your device.
  • Your health data stays on your phone. Medications, doses, side effects, measurements, nutrition logs, notes and photos are stored only on your device. We do not upload them to our servers, and we do not have servers that hold them.
  • What does leave your device is limited to anonymous crash reports, anonymous usage analytics and in-app purchase information, processed by the service providers listed below.
  • If you contact support, we receive what you choose to send us (for example your email address or WhatsApp number and your message).
  • If you choose to subscribe to email updates, we keep your email address to send you news and offers about the App. Subscribing is optional, and every email has an unsubscribe link. We never link your email address to your health data.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.
  • You are in control. You can delete everything from inside the App at any time, or by uninstalling it.

What This Privacy Policy Covers

Glypax is a self-tracking tool that helps you record and review your GLP-1 medication schedule, doses, side effects, body measurements and daily protein, fiber and water intake. We are not a medical provider, we do not provide medical advice, and we are not a “covered entity” or “business associate” under the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (“HIPAA”). Information you record in the App is therefore not “protected health information” under HIPAA. It may, however, be considered health data, sensitive data or consumer health data under other laws, and we treat it with corresponding care.

“Personal Data” means any information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to a particular individual, including information referred to as “personal information”, “personally identifiable information” or “sensitive personal information” under applicable privacy laws.

How to Delete Your Data

Glypax: GLP-1 Tracker handles your profile and health data strictly locally on your device. This data is not stored on our servers or our service providers' servers. To delete all of it:

  1. Open Glypax → Profile → Clear All Data; or
  2. Uninstall the App from your device.

Either action permanently deletes everything the App stores on your device, including your profile, medication and dose records, side effects, measurements, nutrition logs, notes, photos and settings. This cannot be undone. A copy may remain in device backups you control (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup) until you delete those backups. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

Data that is kept. The steps above do not affect the limited technical data processed by our service providers. This data is pseudonymous and is never linked to your health records. Because the App has no accounts, we generally cannot link this data to you; it is deleted automatically at the end of its retention period:

  • Pseudonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): kept for up to 14 months after your last activity in the App, then deleted.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.
  • Purchase records (RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play): kept as long as necessary to manage subscriptions, handle refunds and meet accounting and legal obligations.
  • Support correspondence and, if you subscribed, email subscription data: kept as described under “Data Retention and Deleting Your Data”.
