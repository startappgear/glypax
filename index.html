A GLP-1 tracker for your whole body

Glypax is a GLP-1 medication tracker app for people taking semaglutide, tirzepatide and other GLP-1 medications. It brings doses, side effects, body measurements, progress photos and nutrition together in one private app.

  • Doses, schedule and reminders

    Log each injection or pill with dose, time and injection site, keep track of your medication supply, and get reminders for your next dose.

  • Estimated active level

    View an estimated active level chart based on the doses and times you log. It's for your reference only, not a measurement or medical advice.

  • Side effects

    Note side effects and how you feel, and review your history over time.

  • Weight and 6 body measurements

    Log weight plus waist, hips, chest, thigh, calf and biceps, and see all your measurements in one place.

  • 6 progress photo views

    Take progress photos from 6 views, including face, skin and hair. Photos stay 100% private on your device.

  • Protein, fiber and water

    Log daily protein, fiber and water and compare them with goals you set.

Frequently asked questions

What is Glypax?

Glypax is a GLP-1 tracker app. It helps you log GLP-1 medication doses and reminders, view an estimated active level chart based on your logged doses, note side effects, log weight and 6 body measurements, take progress photos from 6 views, and log daily protein, fiber and water.

Which GLP-1 medications does Glypax support?

Glypax works with all popular GLP-1 medications, including semaglutide (Ozempic®, Wegovy®, Rybelsus®), tirzepatide (Mounjaro®, Zepbound®), liraglutide (Victoza®, Saxenda®), dulaglutide (Trulicity®) and exenatide (Byetta®, Bydureon® BCise®).

Is Glypax free?

Glypax is free to download and starts with a 7-day free trial. After the trial, it continues as a subscription. You can cancel any time in your Google Play account.

Is Glypax available on Android and iPhone?

Glypax is available now on Android from Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.

Are my health data and progress photos private?

Yes. Your medications, doses, side effects, measurements, nutrition logs, notes and progress photos are stored only on your device. Glypax has no accounts, and we never receive your health data or photos.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Glypax works without an account or sign-up. You can export your data to a file and import it on another device.

Does Glypax give medical advice or calculate doses?

No. Glypax is a personal tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not calculate or recommend medication doses. The estimated active level is an informational chart based on the doses you log, not a measurement. Always follow your healthcare provider's instructions.
